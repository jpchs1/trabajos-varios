Timing clashes inside the system itself

Five points that do not depend on the quotation but on the itinerary as it stands today. The first two have to be resolved for the 24th to be operable.

1

The 24th: Puritama ends and Valle de la Muerte begins at the same time

Puritama runs 09:30 to 13:30 and Valle de la Muerte 13:30 to 15:30. The hot springs are some 30 km from San Pedro, close to an hour on the road: you cannot finish there at 13:30 and be in the valley at 13:30.

Beyond the clash, it would be two hours of sandboarding or quad bikes in the hottest part of a December day, straight after four hours in hot springs.

What we propose: Move Valle de la Muerte into the free-afternoon slot, 15:30 to 17:30. It resolves the clash, leaves real margin after Puritama and takes the quads out of the worst hour.

2

Cejar and Valle de la Muerte do not both fit on the 24th

Cejar is quoted 15:30 to 19:30, four hours. If Valle de la Muerte moves into that slot to fix the previous clash, Cejar does not fit. And if Cejar stays where it was quoted, Valle de la Muerte has to go back to 13:30 and the clash returns.

The afternoon of Wednesday 23 is the only gap where Cejar fits whole: the acclimatization ends at 13:00 and Valle de la Luna starts at 17:30. That is four and a half hours, just enough — you would have to leave as soon as the acclimatization ends. In its favour: Cejar sits at salt-flat level, so it does not undo the day’s acclimatization.

What we propose: Decide first whether Cejar is in. If it is, the afternoon of the 23rd; if not, the 24th sorts itself out with Valle de la Muerte at 15:30. Before requoting it, check the 11 removed services the system flags as restorable: Cejar may be among them.

3

The 24th: fifteen minutes between the Christmas Eve dinner and the astronomy

Dinner runs 20:00 to 22:00 and the private astronomy starts at 22:15. Fifteen minutes to get up from the table and go. It is doable, but the operator has to know in advance.

What we propose: Set the pickup at the restaurant rather than the hotel, or close dinner at 21:30. And confirm the astronomy operates on the night of the 24th.

4

The 26th: twenty-four minutes between Quitor and the transfer

Quitor ends at 10:00 and the transfer to Calama leaves at 10:24. In those 24 minutes you have to come back from the pukará, stop at the hotel, check out and load the luggage.

What we propose: Check out before heading to Quitor and carry the luggage in the vehicle. The transfer then leaves straight from the pukará and 24 minutes is more than enough.

5

The 23rd has two blocks and the 24th has five

The 23rd is left with the morning acclimatization and Valle de la Luna at sunset. The 24th, which is Christmas Eve, piles up Puritama, Valle de la Muerte, the free afternoon, dinner and the astronomy.

The altitude progression justifies Puritama sitting on the 24th, so the imbalance is not fixed by moving it back. It is fixed by whatever moves into the afternoon of the 23rd.

What we propose: The afternoon of the 23rd is the only free space left in the whole programme. Cejar is the natural candidate.
