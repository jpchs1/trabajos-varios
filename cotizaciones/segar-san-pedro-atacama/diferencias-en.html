Differences between the quotation and the schedule received

What matches, what changes day or time, what is missing and what has to be resolved before booking. A line-by-line comparison between quotation COT-2026-0160 and the schedule sent to us.

Ref. COT-2026-0160 Programme 22 – 26 Dec 2026 Travellers 4

What has to be resolved first

Six points, ordered by what breaks the trip if left alone. The first three are blocking: as it stands today, the schedule cannot be operated.

1

24 and 25 December are Christmas Eve and Christmas Day

Two of the four activity days fall on public holidays: Thursday 24 with the quad bikes and Friday 25 with the full-day altiplano trip, which is also the most expensive service in the programme and the hardest to put together.

Operations in San Pedro shrink on those two days. Some operators simply do not run on the 25th, the guides and drivers who do work carry a holiday supplement, and CONAF-administered sites may keep special hours. None of this is impossible, but it is the first thing to confirm and the last thing to leave until the end.

What we do: Ask for availability on the 24th and 25th before anything else, with the holiday supplement in writing. If the 25th is not viable, the full day moves to Wednesday 23 and the rest of the programme reorders around it.

2

The morning of the 22nd does not exist: the flight lands at 18:15

The schedule has an acclimatization block from 09:00 to 13:00 on Tuesday 22 and, on the same day, the transfer from Calama arriving in San Pedro at 19:35. You cannot have a calm morning in San Pedro nine hours before arriving there.

It is also the only line in the schedule that does not say To be booked, so it is most likely a placeholder rather than a requested service.

What we do: Confirm whether the acclimatization belongs on Wednesday 23 in the morning. If it does, a decision is needed on the Pukará de Quitor, which is also on the 23rd and has no time.

3

Rainbow Valley does not fit into the Piedras Rojas day

Friday 25 puts Chaxa, Toconao, Miscanti, Piedras Rojas and Rainbow Valley into a single outing from 08:00 to 18:00.

The first four lie south-east of San Pedro, climbing the eastern rim of the salt flat to over 4,000 metres. Rainbow Valley lies north-west, on the road to Río Grande, in the opposite direction. Putting them in one day adds several hundred kilometres of driving and reduces everything else to drive-past stops.

What we do: Take Rainbow Valley out of the 25th and give it its own half day. Thursday 24 in the morning is the natural gap, before the 13:30 quad bikes — subject to operators running on Christmas Eve.

4

The departure transfer is missing

The schedule has the arrival transfer on the 22nd but not the return to El Loa airport. The quotation does include it, on Saturday 26, at CLP 30,000 per person.

What we do: Send the outbound flight so the time can be set. And if the trip ends before the 26th, say so: it changes the last day of the programme.

5

Wednesday 23 has two activities and only one has a time

The 23rd carries the Valle de la Luna at sunset, 17:30 to 20:45, and the Pukará de Quitor with no time. In the quotation, Quitor sat on Saturday 26 from 08:00 to 10:00.

If the 09:00–13:00 acclimatization also moves there, the day ends up with three blocks and Quitor has nowhere to go.

What we do: Decide the order of the 23rd. Quitor early with the acclimatization after it works; all three blocks together does not.

6

Quitor: you can go on your own, but it is not worth it

This is the question the schedule raises. You can visit the Pukará on your own by paying the community entrance: it is an open site with marked paths.

That said, without a guide it is a hillside with stone walls. What makes the visit worth it is the two hours of interpretation: what each enclosure was, how it fell, and why it is remembered as the last Atacameño stand. The quotation carries it guided, at CLP 75,000 per person plus CLP 6,000 entrance.

What we do: We recommend the guided version. If you prefer self-guided, the CLP 75,000 service comes off and only the entrance fee remains.

In practice the quotation has to be rebuilt almost entirely.
