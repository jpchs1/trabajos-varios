15:30 – 19:30 hrs Cejar Lagoon + Ojos de Tebenquiche + Tebenquiche Lagoon

This classic of the heart of the Atacama salt flat opens with a refreshing swim in the emerald brine of Laguna Piedra (Cejar), where you float effortlessly, then continues to the Ojos de Tebenquiche and closes at Tebenquiche Lagoon and its extraordinary living rocks, colonised for millions of years by extremophiles that chose these improbable ecosystems to survive in, where until their discovery life was thought impossible. Conditions and things to keep in mind

What the price covers

  • Every excursion in the programme with a guide, plus whatever each card lists under Includes.
  • Both airport transfers in private service, El Loa – San Pedro and back.
  • Coordination of the astronomy tour, which a third party operates on a shared basis and which we arrange for you as a courtesy.
  • Park fees are itemised separately on each tour and added into the pack total. The service figure on its own does not include them.

Worth knowing

  • Tickets for Cejar and Tebenquiche, Piedras Rojas and the archaeological tour are bought in advance.
  • Valle de la Luna South currently charges no park fee, but one may be in force by your travel date.
  • Transfer times are still to be coordinated and confirmed against your flights.
  • The first two alternatives are quoted as a minimum group rate on a 4-traveller basis: with a different party size the per-person figure changes.
  • 24 and 25 December are Christmas Eve and Christmas Day: guide and service availability on those two days has to be confirmed first, and a holiday supplement may apply.
  • Everything remains subject to availability at the time of confirmation.
