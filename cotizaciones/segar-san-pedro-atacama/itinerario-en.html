Proposed itinerary

Times we need confirmed

The itinerary we propose to run from 22 to 26 December, with the agreed adjustments: Cejar moves to the morning of the 23rd as the acclimatization, Quitor and Valle de la Muerte go together on the afternoon of the 24th, and the 26th is left free. No day carries more than two outings.

The 4 adjustments

Everything else stays on the day and time the client has already seen in the system.

1

Cejar moves to the morning of the 23rd and becomes the acclimatization

Depart the hotel at 10:15 to be at the complex by 10:50, back at 14:15. The block that said “acclimatization” becomes this: floating at Laguna Piedra is exactly how you spend a first full day without exerting yourself.

It also orders the altitude: the 23rd at salt-flat level (2,300 m), the 24th at Puritama (3,500 m) and the 25th at Miscanti (above 4,000 m). The complex closes on Tuesdays; the only Tuesday of the trip is the 22nd, the arrival day, which carries no excursions.

2

Quitor and Valle de la Muerte go together on the afternoon of the 24th

A single private half day from 14:30 to 18:30: Quitor first, twenty minutes on the road, and sandboarding at Valle de la Muerte at the end. They are fifteen minutes apart, so it is one vehicle and one guide instead of two outings.

It is also what the client had already decided — keeping Quitor and Valle de la Muerte together — and it is what frees up the 26th.

3

The 26th is left free

No excursions. Free morning, check-out, and at 10:24 the transfer to Calama for the 13:44 flight.

4

The afternoon of the 23rd and the evening of the 22nd are left free

On the 22nd you reach the hotel at 19:15 and nothing else is scheduled. On the 23rd there are three hours between Cejar and Valle de la Luna. No day in the programme carries more than two outings.

What we need you to confirm

The first four are cross-cutting; the rest follow the itinerary line by line.

  1. Availability and holiday supplement for 24 and 25 DecemberChristmas Eve and Christmas Day, with the altiplano full day on the 25th. First thing to close: if the 25th cannot operate, the programme gets rebuilt.
  2. Rates for 4 travellersThe original quote was built on 2 for the trekking alternatives, at a minimum group rate. We need the figure for 4.
  3. Vehicle for the full dayWith 4 travellers plus guide-driver and luggage on a ten-hour run up to the altiplano, which vehicle would you assign?
  4. Rainbow Valley: it does not fit todayThe client had asked for it. With the 26th free and every other day full, there is no space without dropping something else. Do you run it as a half day of its own, and at what time? That way the client decides with the facts in hand.
  5. 22 Dec · Transfer Calama → San Pedro de AtacamaPickup at Calama airport on arrival of the 17:41 LATAM flight.
  6. 23 Dec · Laguna Cejar complex · floating at Laguna Piedra + Ojos de Tebenquiche + Tebenquiche LagoonDeparture 10:15 from the hotel to arrive 10:50 at the complex: is that the right drive time? Does the full circuit (Piedra, Ojos and Tebenquiche) close at 14:15? And please confirm the complex opens on Wednesday the 23rd.
  7. 23 Dec · Valle de la Luna at sunsetIs a 17:30 start enough for the off-circuit route through El Vallecito, which is walked? That is the one quoted.
  8. 24 Dec · Pukará de Quitor + Valle de la Muerte with sandboardingDo you run it as a combined half day in that order and time frame? Quitor is quoted guided at 2 h and here it runs in 1 h 30. Sandboarding is quoted; quad bikes are not and we do not operate them.
  9. 24 Dec · Private astronomyThe moon that night is 99% illuminated and no other night of the trip is better. How do you handle that on a private tour, aimed at the moon and planets? And please confirm the 22:10 pickup at the restaurant. What is quoted is the shared tour: the private one needs quoting.
  10. 25 Dec · Altiplano · Chaxa, Toconao, Miscanti and Piedras RojasPlease confirm the scope: Chaxa comes in, with its own entrance fee, and Tuyajto and the proto-villages drop out from the quote. How does the price land and which tickets are bought in advance?

Is the programme feasible at these times? We look forward to closing it and holding the spaces.
