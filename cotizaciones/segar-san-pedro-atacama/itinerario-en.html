Proposed itinerary

Times we need confirmed

The itinerary we propose to run from 22 to 26 December, with the agreed adjustments: Cejar moves to the morning of the 23rd as the acclimatization, Quitor and Valle de la Muerte go together on the afternoon of the 24th, and the 26th is left free. No day carries more than two outings.

Ref. COT-2026-0160 Programme 22 – 26 Dec 2026 Travellers 4

The 6 adjustments

Everything else stays on the day and time the client has already seen in the system.

1

Cejar can only be operated in the afternoon

Agencies cannot enter Cejar in the morning, so the 10:15 departure we had built does not exist. It runs Wednesday 23 from 15:30 to 19:30, the slot the operator proposed.

The idea of the 23rd being a quiet day still holds: the morning carries only two hours at Quitor and there are almost four free hours before heading out to the salt flat.

2

Vallecito leaves at 17:00, and so it moves to the 24th

The operator warns that leaving at 17:30 does not allow doing it properly: the latest is 17:00, and the excursion runs 17:00 to 21:00.

With that, Cejar (15:30 – 19:30) and Vallecito (17:00 – 21:00) overlap and can no longer share a day. Cejar stays on the 23rd — that way the 21:00 astronomy has an hour and a half of buffer — and Vallecito moves to the 24th.

3

The astronomy goes back to shared and to the 23rd

The private tour on the 24th costs CLP 600,000 for the group. The shared one already quoted is CLP 40,000 per person, and it is the night Segar asked about: it lands on the 23rd, 21:00 to 23:00.

Something worth knowing beforehand: that night the moon is 99.9% illuminated, and the 24th is the same. Switching nights changes nothing, so the right move is for the guide to aim it at the moon and planets.

4

Quitor goes on the morning of the 23rd, and bills separately

The operator makes clear that Quitor and the sandboarding are two separate excursions, even done within the same half day. Since the 26th has to stay free and both afternoons go to Cejar and Vallecito, the only gap left is the morning of the 23rd.

Two hours a step from the village, so it does not weigh on the settling-in day.

5

The transfers take 1 h 45

Not the long hour we had. It changes both ends of the trip: on the 22nd you reach the hotel at 20:00 rather than 19:15, and on the 26th the transfer moves up to 10:00 to reach Calama at 11:45, two hours before the 13:44 flight.

6

What Segar has to decide: the time of the Christmas Eve dinner

Vallecito ends at 21:00 and dinner was set for 20:00. Either dinner moves to 21:30 — which for Christmas Eve in Chile is the normal hour — or Valle de la Luna cannot go on the 24th, and it would have to come out of the programme: there is no other afternoon for it.

The transfer to dinner is no longer needed: Segar confirmed they will use the hotel shuttle.
