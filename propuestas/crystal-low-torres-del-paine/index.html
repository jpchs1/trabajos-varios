Two things we had to change — and why

Winter calendar check
  • Tierra Patagonia is closed on your dates. The hotel shuts from 1 May to 30 September every year — mid-August falls squarely inside that window. We'd rather tell you now than have you find out at check-in.
  • Explora Torres del Paine is open, and it's the closest thing to Tierra there is. Same design-lodge spirit, floor-to-ceiling glass onto the massif — and it sits inside the park at Salto Chico rather than looking in from outside. It's one of only two hotels in the park that stay open through winter.
  • The Grey Glacier boat does not sail on Sundays in winter. From May to August the Grey III runs on Wednesdays and Saturdays only, one departure, 10:00–13:45. Your draft had it on Sunday 16th — it would not have happened.
  • Saturday 15 August is a sailing day. So we moved the navigation to Saturday morning and rebuilt the rest of the weekend around it. You keep the glacier.

The one thing we'd ask of you: fly down to Punta Arenas on Friday morning instead of Friday evening. An evening arrival means a five-hour drive in the dark and a night lost in Puerto Natales. A morning flight puts you at the lodge before sunset — and buys you a second sunrise, which is the thing you said you came for.

Note too that Puerto Natales airport only takes commercial flights in high season, so Punta Arenas (PUQ) is the only way in in August.

The detail

Not included

  • Domestic flights. Santiago → Punta Arenas and Punta Arenas → Puerto Montt. We book them for you at cost once you confirm.
  • Park entrance (CONAF), paid at the gate — roughly USD 22 per person in low season.
  • Lunches, dinners and drinks outside the hotel.
  • Travel insurance and anything personal.

Things to know

  • Sunday's flight north needs checking. A direct Punta Arenas → Puerto Montt in the evening is not always in the schedule; the routing may go via Santiago. We'll confirm before you book anything.
  • Winter weather decides. The navigation and the mountain roads are subject to wind and snow. If Grey is cancelled we refund that leg in full and walk you to the Grey lookout instead.
  • Daylight is about 9h 40. Sunrise 08:40, sunset 18:20 — the sun stays low all day, which is why the light is so good.
  • If Friday morning is impossible: we move Night 1 to Puerto Natales and you lose one sunrise. Everything else holds.
