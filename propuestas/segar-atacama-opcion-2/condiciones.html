Paying

Everything must be received and confirmed 24 hours before your first service. Your first service is the transfer from Calama on Tuesday 22 December, so the money has to be sitting in their account on Monday 21 December. A booking that is not paid by then is cancelled automatically — there is no grace period written anywhere.

An international transfer in Christmas week is not a same-day thing. Start it early enough that a slow bank cannot cost you the trip, and send us the confirmation as soon as you have it.

If they have to cancel a day

Weather, a road closed for safety, a decision by the park authority. If it happens you choose one of three: another date, a different outing of the same value, or that day's money back.

Said honestly: on a four-day trip "another date" often does not exist — there is no spare day to move Friday into. And beyond those three options there is no compensation. That is their position, and it is normal in the trade.

What we do about it

If a day falls over, sorting it out is not your job. We write to you the same day with concrete options — part of the value back, an upgrade on something you already have, or a credit — and you pick the one you prefer. You will not be standing in a hotel lobby negotiating in Spanish.

Altitude · please answer this one before we book

Friday 25 December spends several hours above 4,000 metres — Piedras Rojas and the altiplanic lagoons. Puritama on Thursday sits near 3,500. San Pedro itself is 2,400.

The operator states they do not recommend going above 3,500 metres for anyone with a history of heart disease, diabetes, epilepsy, asthma or high blood pressure, and the guide makes the final call on the day — they can ask a passenger to sit an activity out.

So: does any of that apply to any of the four of you? Tell us now, in confidence. There is a good version of this trip either way — Friday can be reshaped, or swapped for something lower — but we need to know while it can still be changed, not on the morning of the 25th at the pick-up.

Flights, and why insurance is not optional here

Your arrival transfer is timed to your flight into Calama. If that flight is delayed or cancelled, the transfer is lost and is not refunded — their terms exclude it explicitly, and the same goes for any outing you miss because of it. Medical, rescue and hospital costs during the tours are yours as well: if an evacuation is ever needed, the guide can call it, and the bill follows the passenger.

None of this is unusual. It is simply the case for travel insurance with proper medical and trip-interruption cover, and it is worth buying before you pay the balance rather than after.
