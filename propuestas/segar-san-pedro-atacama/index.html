What we checked, and what came out of it

December calendar check
  • Thursday 24 December is Christmas Eve, and you get back to town at 20:30. In Chile the celebration is the night of the 24th, not the morning of the 25th. Kitchens in San Pedro either close early that evening or run a single set menu that fills up weeks ahead. Coming off the Valle de la Luna at half past eight with nothing booked is the one way this week goes wrong. Tell us what you want and we will book it now — hotel set menu, a table in town, or a private dinner.
  • Thursday is labelled "full day" in the operator's programme. It is not, and that is in your favour. Puritama runs 09:30–13:30 and the Valle de la Luna Sur runs 16:30–20:30 — two half-days with three free hours between them, and the price quoted is the half-day rate, not a full-day one. We have listed it correctly below so there is no surprise at the desk.
  • The altitude is sequenced correctly. Friday climbs to roughly 4,500 m at Piedras Rojas and the altiplanic lagoons. It falls on your fourth day, after three nights at 2,400 m in San Pedro — which is exactly where it belongs. We would not move it earlier, and we would not add anything to Thursday night.
  • Three of the four park entries have to be bought in advance. Cejar, Puritama and Piedras Rojas sell tickets ahead of the date and the operator must hold them — which is why the full ticket cost, CLP 284,000, sits in the confirmation payment rather than being paid on the day.

And the one thing that is not in our hands: this quotation is subject to availability. Nothing in it — not the vehicles, not the excursions, not the tickets for the 24th and 25th — is held until the agency has your confirmation. December is the busiest month of the year in San Pedro and those two dates are the hardest in it; the diary closes early and it closes for everyone at once. If it closes before you confirm, we do not get these dates back. We would be going out to a different operator, at a different price, and the week you have just read would not be the week you get. Given the lead times, this needs closing now.

Confirming early costs you nothing, and that is worth saying plainly: the operator returns the whole deposit on any cancellation made 90 days or more before departure, which for 22 December means on or before Wednesday 23 September. Say yes now and you take the dates off the market while your money is still entirely recoverable.

Things to know

  • Entrance fees are shown at the foreign-visitor rate. Several of these parks charge Chilean nationals less. If anyone in the party holds a Chilean ID, tell us and the entries come down.
  • There are reduced rates for children at three of the four parks — under 18 at Arcoíris and Piedras Rojas, under 13 at Cejar. We have priced all four of you as adults; tell us if that is wrong and the total drops.
  • Cejar is intensely saline. Rinse showers are on site. Bring a towel, flip-flops, and nothing you mind getting salt on.
  • December is high summer here. Strong sun and around 25 °C in town by day; close to freezing at 4,500 m on Friday morning. Layers, hat, high-factor sunscreen.
  • Excursion times are the operator's. They can shift with weather or road conditions in the altiplano; the agency will tell you the night before.
