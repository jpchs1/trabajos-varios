Postponement and cancellation policy

The operator's terms

Our reservations are materialised well in advance, which normally means that our diary fills up early and no longer admits new entries. For this reason, reservations may be postponed at no additional cost if the postponement is made at least 60 days before the programme begins — in this case the deposit paid to confirm remains intact for the next date.

Reservations that are to be cancelled will have 100% of the deposit returned, provided the cancellation is made at least 90 days in advance. If the cancellation is made between 90 and 60 days, the return will be 50%. After that period there is no right to a refund.
